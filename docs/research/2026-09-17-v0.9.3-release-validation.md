# v0.9.3 Release Validation

Date: 2026-09-17. Rebuilt: 2026-09-18.

The release adopts the owner-approved R2 scoring scheme for all 71 numeric fields
in Property Calculation Basic and Advanced. The
[approval record](../design/property-calculation-r2-scoring.md) distinguishes
formal adoption from independent scientific calibration. Gold values, field
profiles, task IDs, and RDKit/xTB scoring behavior are unchanged. The rebuild
incorporates the owner-reviewed Advanced 002 aggregation: the two phase strings
form one all-or-nothing exact-match decision worth half of the score, and the
potential-energy difference is worth the other half.
