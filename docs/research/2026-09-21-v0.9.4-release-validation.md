# v0.9.4 Release Validation

v0.9.4 formalizes the public `family-policy.yaml` and R3 scoring policy.
`noncovalent_energy` uses a `2 kcal/mol` zero-score width. Advanced A008 and
A013 require preserving energy sign; A010 distances use signed identity scoring
with a nonnegative domain. The family policy is packaged and validated against
all Property Calculation numeric profiles by the task loader.
